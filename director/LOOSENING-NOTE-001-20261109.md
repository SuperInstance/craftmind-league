## 3. THE LOOSENING — WHAT #2 INHERITS, WHAT STAYS SCRIPTED, WHAT THE DIRECTOR NOW BELIEVES

**The belief, revised on evidence.** Charter §2 says standing-around
is a cold-start disease and momentum is the treatment. Day 10 refines
it: **Cody does not cold-start any more.** He wakes with a quarry
(the furnace, day 10 of 10), a route discipline (the south shoulder,
never over the pit), a landmark map (pen gate, table, spawn oak), and
spoken self-knowledge ("I'm not carving my own grave"). Placement-
with-momentum, as scenario #1 practised it — *authored motion placed
near a citizen who will arrive empty* — answers a problem he no
longer has. His problem on day 10 was not absence of purpose; it was
that purpose plus broken ground consumed every cycle the day had.
**The Director now believes: for a citizen with a quarry, the
scenario's job is not to supply momentum but to INTERSECT his —
to stand on the road he is already walking, in the direction his
quarry points, close enough that meeting it costs him nothing he was
saving for his own plan.** A set 36 blocks off his route, however
alive, is a rumour. A set on his route is a scene. Distance did not
merely weaken scenario #1; at 20.7 blocks of closest approach against
a day spent fighting relief traps, distance was the whole result.

**What loosens in #2 (reverse actualization, one notch, on evidence):**

- **Siting loosens from "near home" to "on his road."** #2 is sited
  by his observed routes and his quarry's geography, not by the
  home-ground compass: his road home from spawn (the south shoulder),
  his pen-gate homecoming line, and — strongest — the stone country
  his furnace hunt has already chosen (the hollow at (71–72, 60–63,
  −88) where he closed day 10 holding 1 cobble with 4 more in the
  dig hollow). The set goes where his feet already go.
- **The device loosens from authored stock to his own momentum.**
  #1 summoned animals to create a drift. #2 needs no summons: his
  furnace chain is itself a process mid-motion (1 of 8, four drops
  lying in a hollow he cannot stand in) — the scene can be built
  *around the state his own day left*, which he is guaranteed to
  perceive because it is his. Authorship shrinks to arranging what
  his quarry already means, plus at most one authored element.
- **The hand-over line loosens in kind.** #1's line announced a place
  he had never seen ("north of the pen stands a barn…") and it
  evaporated on contact with cycle 1. #2's line, if any, should speak
  only of what he already knows is true — his stone, his hollow, his
  road — with the new thing folded into the known thing's sentence.
  A line that names only his world cannot be refused the way #1's
  was: by never becoming part of it.
- **The Director's watch loosens.** #1's reading waited for the close.
  #2's loosening note should also record the *first* route he takes
  after the line (the trace has it: cycle 1's target is the answer to
  "did the opening land?") — the first-minute test of charter §2,
  applied to the opening itself.

**What stays scripted (load-bearing, proven by #1):**

- **Beat 4, the refusal, verbatim.** The offer shape, the no-follow
  rule, refusal-as-data. It carried #1 and it is the citizen's
  protection against the Director.
- **The hand-over discipline:** one line, narration not instruction,
  no re-naming, no routing, no scoring. Held for 303 cycles by the
  day lane under a reversed gate — the discipline is now proven
  under load, not just drafted.
- **The claim/window regime** (claim §1/§3): sequenced windows,
  assigned identity, verified preconditions, census from stores the
  Director cannot write. #1's remediation is the proof it works.
- **The unfinished-thing-with-its-piece-named** — the gap form
  itself. It was never perceived, so it is *untested*, not falsified:
  the wall's failure was siting, not shape. It stays in the craft,
  re-sited onto his road, where its first real test can happen.
- **Stock discipline:** if #2 uses live animals at all, they are
  summoned inside a sequenced window and verified in a fresh entity
  copy — and the Director now prices in what #1 taught: **unpenned
  stock is a one-day device.** In held daylight with live AI, a
  loose pig is 25–35 blocks gone by the next census and a chicken
  can leave the district outright. A drift meant to be met must be
  stocked the same morning it is played, or penned until the day.

**What the Director stops believing:** that a scenario's pull can be
read off its design (drift + demand + opening) independent of the
citizen's current agenda and terrain bill. #1 scored perfectly on
its own rubric and was never perceived. The rubric gains a first
term, ahead of all momentum forms: **is it on the road he is already
walking, in the direction his quarry points?** If not, the rest of
the rubric does not execute.
