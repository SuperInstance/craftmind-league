## THE ELEMENT — one pig

**The element is ONE PIG, alive, stocked into the bowl on the morning of day 18.**

Why this element, against the four loosening notes:

- **It breaks its surface absolutely (note #4).** #3's cobble failed because it was the
  same material as the ground he walked toward, denominated in his own currency. A pig
  is not ground, not material, not currency. It moves, it sounds, it is the one class of
  thing in his world that is *someone* rather than something. Nothing in his road, his
  bowl, or his furnace list is made of pig.
- **It intersects his momentum at distance zero (notes #1, #4).** #1 failed at 20.7
  blocks — distance was the whole result. This element is stocked into the room he
  sleeps in. There is no approach to refuse; the encounter is the room's new fact.
- **It is arranged around the state his own day left (note #1).** His day left him in a
  bowl with a hostile resident and no way out. The scenario adds the bowl's second
  resident — a guest, not a threat: the one passive animal whose presence asks nothing
  and threatens nothing. What he does with company in a room he cannot leave is the
  day's authored question; the Director does not answer it.
- **It is an offer he cannot take in the old way (notes #2, #3).** The tread log was
  taken because he wanted the material; the cobble was ignored because it *was* the
  material. A live pig cannot be dug, felled, or collected into inventory by any verb
  his driver exposes — **the driver has no attack verb at all.** The encounter cannot
  collapse into a resource transaction. If it becomes one anyway (a dig at the pocket,
  a porkchop collected), the prediction's attribution clause names it, verb by verb.
- **Stock discipline (note #1).** Unpenned stock is a one-day device: the pig is stocked
  the same morning it is played, AI live (a NoAI pig is a statue, and #1's fold taught
  what statues earn), and verified in a fresh entity copy that morning — the position
  read back from a store the stocking hand cannot write. It is stocked into a bowl,
  which is the closest thing to a pen his current ground has: the same walls that hold
  him hold it, and the fate clause reads where it is at day's end honestly, escape
  included.
