## F1 — The reconciliation breach: diagnosed, then fixed in the matcher

### The symptom

At ledger impl the reconciliation read: trace governor cycles 379 vs governor
`cody-driver` receipts 393 → deviation +14 = **+3.7%, within the ±5% tolerance**
(study D.3). After the feeds-completion rebuild it read: trace cycles 524 vs
receipts 608 → **+84 = +16.0%, `within_tolerance_5pct: false`** (FEEDS-IMPL §3, F1).

### The diagnosis — arithmetic first

The matcher (`feeds/governor-feed.js`, pre-fix) had an asymmetry built into its
two sides:

- **Numerator:** ALL `cody-driver` receipt rows in `ledger/mind-calls.jsonl` —
  every status, in-span and outside-span alike (608).
- **Denominator:** governor-routed *cycle rows* summed over the traced days'
  spans (524).

Decomposing the 608 receipts by status and span (receipts carry a `status`
field; every figure below reproduces from `ledger/mind-calls.jsonl` and the
trace files):

| Class | Rows | Where |
|---|---:|---|
| Receipts served (status 200), inside a trace span | 535 | days 1 (+5 over cycles), 2 (exact), 3 (exact), 5 (+6), 12 (exactly the day's 6 governor cycles), 15 (exactly 79), 16 (exactly 60); zero in days 4, 6, 7, 9, 10, 11, 13, 14 |
| Receipts served (status 200), outside every trace span | 3 | 2026-11-09T06:23:30Z (before day 1's span), 08:26:00Z and 08:38:17Z (between the day-2 and day-3 spans) — the impl-era named class: probes / wake checks |
| Receipts rejected (status 400), inside a trace span | **70** | **all 70 inside day 12's span**, 2026-11-10T04:33:36.127Z → 04:37:30.479Z (3 min 54 s), `total_tokens: null` on every row |
| Receipts rejected, outside spans | 0 | — |
| **Total** | **608** | served 538, rejected 70 |

The trace side, over the same days: governor-routed cycle rows = 524
(day 1: 57, day 2: 26, day 3: 237, day 5: 59, day 12: 6, day 15: 79,
day 16: 60; zero elsewhere).

The +84 decomposes exactly:

```
+84  =  +70  rejected receipts (day-12 storm — a class that can never be cycles)
     +  +11  in-span served surplus (day 1: +5, day 5: +6 — probes/failover checks,
             the impl filing's own named explanation, unchanged since impl)
     +   +3  served receipts outside every span (probes/wake checks)
```

**The cause, named (brief's candidate (b), with a (c) rider):** receipts that
were never driver cycles entered the receipt count — specifically the 70
HTTP-400 rejections of day 12's late failover. They are governor *attempts the
route refused*, in the trace's own last minutes: the primary's 37 model-fail
rows begin at ~cycle 324 (04:29Z, where the 6 served governor cycles land,
04:29:14Z–04:33:03Z); at 04:33:36Z the route starts answering 400, and the
driver retries against it until the window closes at 04:37:35Z. The trace
files no cycle row for a refused call — correctly — so the receipts existed
with no possible cycle counterpart. Candidate (a) is excluded: the span
matching is right (day 12's served in-span receipts equal its trace governor
cycles exactly, 6 = 6; days 15/16 exact at 79/79 and 60/60; days 2/3 exact).
The (c) rider is real but small and pre-existing: the +11 in-span served
surplus (days 1/5) and the +3 outside-span served receipts are genuine
receipts with no cycle rows — probes and failover checks — named at impl and
unchanged; they are a class, not an error.

**Why the breach appeared only now:** at impl the receipt total was 393 and
the storm did not exist; the feeds extension added days 11–16, and exactly one
of those days (12) had a rejection storm inside its span (+70) while its
served side matched its cycles exactly. The feeds lane's own guess — "receipts
outside every trace span grew the numerator" — is measured here to be worth
+3 of +84; the named cause in its filing was the minor term.

### The fix — reconcile by class, never by forcing agreement

`feeds/governor-feed.js` `reconcile()` now splits both books into named classes
and applies the D.3 ±5% test to the only pair that is the same kind of thing:
**receipts served (status 200) vs trace governor cycles**, globally, as at impl.
Rejected receipts are reported as their own class (they are attempts, never
cycles); served receipts outside spans keep their impl-era name; a
`cycles_unreceipted_in_span` class is computed per day (currently 0 on every
day — every governor cycle has a served receipt inside its span). The old
all-receipts headline is retained in the output as
`legacy_all_receipts_deviation` so the +16.0% stays explainable rather than
vanishing. Neither book was edited: `mind-calls.jsonl` and every trace file
are byte-untouched.

### After the fix (rebuild output, `out/per-day.json` → `governor_reconciliation`)

| Measure | Before (feeds rebuild) | After (this rebuild) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Headline deviation (the D.3 test) | +84 = +16.0%, within tolerance: **false** | **+14 = +2.7%, within tolerance: true** |
| Receipts served vs trace cycles | (mixed into the 608) | 538 vs 524 = +14 (day 1 +5, day 5 +6, outside-span +3; days 2, 3, 12, 15, 16 exact) |
| Receipts rejected (named class) | counted as deviation | 70, all in day 12's span, reported separately |
| Cycles unreceipted in spans | not computed | 0 |

The after-fix headline (+14, +2.7%) is the impl-era figure (+14, +3.7%) with
the denominator grown by the new valid governor days — the same explained
surplus, reconciled. The two books now agree *by explanation*: every one of
the 608 receipts and 524 cycles is in exactly one named class.

## F2 — Validity rulings for days 11–16 (study D.4, step 1)

**Criteria, as written in the study (D.4 step 1) and carried in `build.js`:**
a day is valid iff (1) no dead relay, (2) no untraced route change,
(3) active minutes ≥ 30, and (4) the citizen was not stopped by harness fault
for most of it. The study's own named inspections (days 3 and 5) stand as the
precedent for regime exclusions. Active minutes are the trace feed's measure
(first→last cycle row), the same measure the impl rulings used (day 9's 7.1).

A note on what was ruled on: the day-11 trace file pools the Gate B STOP
attempt (3 `gate-refuse-start` rows, zero cycles) with the played relaunch.
The STOP attempt is not a citizen day and is not ruled on; the ruling below is
on the played span. Day 17 has no trace (never launched) and day 8 none
(never woken) — nothing exists to rule on, as at impl.

| Day | Cycles | β (wc/min) | Active min | Ruling |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 11 | 31 | 4.152 | 7.47 | **EXCLUDED** |
| 12 | 327 | 3.975 | 80.9 | **VALID, flagged** |
| 13 | 209 | 4.376 | 47.8 | **EXCLUDED** |
| 14 | 32 | 3.168 | 10.1 | **EXCLUDED** |
| 15 | 268 | 2.316 | 85.0 | **VALID, flagged** |

  criterion 4: the floor's PARK (`daylight_unrestorable`, 01:07:03Z) stopped
  the citizen at 7.5 minutes, and the day-11 rig repair later proved the
  park's premise false — the three daylight commands landed (server log + op
  results ok:true) and the floor verified none, an off-by-one in its mirror
  arithmetic. A harness fault stopped the citizen; the day is a fragment, in
  the same two-criterion failure shape as day 9. (Its one route was single
  and traced; the relay death that day was pre-launch and recovered — neither
  saves it, neither is charged.)
- **Day 12 — VALID, flagged.** All four criteria pass: relay death was
  pre-check, recovered, RELAY-OK at check and at launch, 22/22 daylight

  finding: the stop was the safety system working, the route was single and

  minutes, exit rc=0, 237 cycle rows, zero deaths, never stopped by harness

## The β₀ consequence — computed, whatever it is

The last-three-valid set changes. Days 1, 2, 4, 10 remain valid (impl
rulings untouched); days 12, 15, 16 join them; the last three valid days are
now **12, 15, 16**.

| Quantity | Before F2 | After F2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Baseline window | [2, 4, 10] | **[12, 15, 16]** |
| β by window day | 3.976 / 4.782 / 3.575 | 3.975 / 2.316 / 2.166 |
| **β₀ (median)** | **3.976** | **2.316 wc/min** |
| Noise band (min–max) | 3.575 – 4.782 | 2.166 – 3.975 |
| β∞ (accounting target) | 1.252 | 0.699 |
| β_floor | 1.186 (novelty binds) | 1.000 (**liveness now binds**; novelty term fell to 0.649) |
| β_next (operative ceiling) | 3.976 | **2.316** |
| Day budget B (85 min, A = GAP as ever) | 337.96 wc | **196.89 wc** |
| Hard cap (200%) | 675.92 wc | 393.77 wc |
| C (compilable share, wc) | 0.761 | 0.776 (33 keys, was 31) |
| Credit / budget falls | 0 / no | 0 / no — the fall in β_next is a **re-baseline**, not compilation credit; no asset retired anything |

**β₀ MOVED: 3.976 → 2.316 wc/min.** Read it for what it is: the median valid
day is now a two-regime blend day. Days 15/16 ran roughly a quarter to a
third of their cycles on the fallback tier at weight 0.1 — the frozen-weight
arithmetic prices those cycles cheap, and their β falls accordingly. That is
the study's design working as written (a fallback-heavy day *is* a cheaper
day in wc), and both days carry the blend flag in their rulings so the number
is never quoted bare. It is NOT a compilation gain: C moved 0.761 → 0.776,
credit is 0, no proven asset retired any share (the furnace asset's
`retired_share` remains 0; its `proven` flag is the build's ≥5-uses bar, as
the feeds filing already noted without ruling).

**Consequences that follow and are stated, not smoothed:**

1. **The day-18 charter was drafted under the old ceiling.** Its budget
   section (§7 as filed) reads calls and rate against "the 3.976 wc/min
   ceiling." Under the new baseline the operative ceiling is **2.316 wc/min**
   and the 85-minute day budget is **196.89 wc** (A = GAP unchanged). **The
   day-18 run lane must file a budget addendum on these numbers before the
   run** — this lane rules and computes; it does not amend the charter.
2. **The gate fixtures re-ruled themselves.** Re-running
   `fixtures/run-fixtures.js` against the new budget flips fixture (a) — day
   10 replayed as a plan — from PASS to **OVER-TARGET** (β_plan 3.573 >
   ceiling 2.316), because day 10 is no longer inside the window it is ruled
   against. Fixtures (b) and (c) are unchanged in kind (BELOW-FLOOR, now
   against the liveness floor 1.000; OVER-TARGET + hard-cap). This is the
   gate working as designed — a re-baseline re-prices history — and it is
   filed here so the flip is a record, not a surprise in someone else's run.
3. **β∞ (0.699) now sits BELOW β_floor (1.000).** At impl, β∞ (1.252) sat
   barely above the novelty floor (1.186) and the filing noted the floor
   would bind "almost immediately" if compilation approached the target.
   The re-baseline has overtaken that note: the accounting target is under
   the floor, so if compilation ever did approach it, the liveness floor
   binds first and the schedule's max() is what a real day would be priced
   against. Filed as arithmetic, not alarm.
