## 2. THE DIG-GUARD — a harness change, chartered here (not smuggled)

The extraction investigation's recommendation (1), implemented in
the day-13 driver copy in the existing pit-guard pattern:

- **Arming.** The guard arms at the stone phase's first
  stone-family dig (stone, cobblestone, andesite, diorite, granite,
  deepslate, cobbled deepslate, tuff). The ENTRY level is the
  stance's feet level at that moment, trace-rowed
  (`kind: 'dig-guard-arm'`, reason `stone-phase-entry`). A stone dig
  issued more than 6 blocks from the arm point re-arms at the new
  stance (reason `new-stance`) — entry is a stance's fact, not a
  day's.
- **The refusal.** While armed, ANY dig whose target cell's TOP is
  more than 2 levels below the entry level (target top < entry−2)
  is refused BEFORE execution. A refused dig is trace-rowed
  (`kind: 'dig-guard'`, carrying the refused target, its block, the
  entry, the cap, his feet, and the running refusal count) and the
  model is told why in its own result line, naming the entry and
  the cap and day 12's shaft. Nothing is silent; nothing is
  re-aimed — the guard refuses, as the pit guard's refusal half
  does, and the model's plan stays its own.
- **Disarming.** When a dig is next considered with read-back
  cobblestone ≥ 8 in his inventory, the guard disarms
  (`kind: 'dig-guard-disarm'`, reason `count-closed`): the stone
  phase is over and the cap no longer binds anything.
- **Scope.** The guard exists only in the day-13 driver copy. It
  does not touch goto (the pit guard's ground), climb, place, or
  any dig above the cap.
